# Gameplay design choices

Recovered design intent for Better Content: what the world should make possible, what choices should matter, and why its systems fit together. This is not an inventory of proven runtime behavior. The companion [Aspirational features](aspirational-features.md) separates future proposals, unfinished extensions and unresolved decisions from these established directions.

## A world of interacting causes

Better Content is an expert pack built around systems natural to its world. **Matter, Place and Life** make expected reality mechanically present; **Blood, Fonts and Traces Into Lineage** introduce distinct supernatural premises. **History** is persistence through play. **Society** emerges from actors, resources and consequences.

Technology, magic and adventure are player-facing packages, not separate causal universes. Geological resources, processing, bodily needs, spirit markets, dimensional expeditions, discoveries and death connect them. A magical route may solve a physical problem differently without inventing a second, incompatible material economy.

The world should reward understanding and preparation rather than collecting abstract permission tokens. Materials can be shared or traded. Personal exploration flags are not a substitute for possessing the actual capability or resource.

## Finite matter, bounded space and coherent alternatives

- Nonliving matter should consume finite substrate. Biological growth and breeding can remain renewable.
- Storage may be powerful, expensive and spatially unusual, but not infinite or universally accessible from anywhere.
- Parallel routes should offer distinct costs and affordances, not duplicate every system or erase its constraints.
- Conventional modular tools belong to Tinkers' Construct. Unique weapons, bows, armor, creature gear and material integrations can retain separate identities. Wood is useful for handles, bindings and other fittings, not tool heads.
- PowerGrid provides stationary electrical generation. Kinetic power, pressure, heat, gas networks, storage, electrical consumers and computation remain distinct engineering systems.
- Vanilla potion delivery is removed as a redundant family. Plain water, food effects, status effects and independently owned flask systems remain meaningful.
- The vanilla End is inaccessible. Needed End-like materials and ecology can have specifically chosen alternatives in orbit, cultivation or dragon ecology; that does not imply relocating every End-exclusive item.

The old design explicitly acknowledged incomplete closure of finite matter: vanilla water/lava renewal and renewable stone loops remained exceptions. A strict finite-fluid replacement was deferred, not already achieved.

“Muted” content can fit the pack while lacking ordinary survival acquisition. The four End-only Starcatcher catches—Charfish, Chorus Crab, End Glow and Voidbiter—were deliberately left End-bound. Existing specimens and their uses were not removed. Muting is neither deletion nor a promise of a future unlock.

## Progression through manufacturing and place

Six manufacturing eras provide a broad spine, but each capability follows its own material and manufacturing requirements. Opening one category does not impose every other category's gate.

| Stage | Intended capability and character |
| --- | --- |
| Primitive workshop | Handworked wood, rock, flint, bone, hides, fibers and rope; hearth materials; simple copper/iron fittings; local carrying, storage and transport |
| 1. Hand workshop and Tinkers' Construct | Hand separation of geological chunks, underground-rock refractory materials, modular tools and Seared metallurgy |
| 2. Create powered works | Iron-fitted presses and mixers; powered separation; scalable Certus-rooted physical logistics |
| 3. Create precision and steam | Brass composition, precision mechanisms, steam, rail systems and high-throughput separation |
| 4. PneumaticCraft and Heat Sync | Pressure, sealed chemistry, process heat, maximum geological recovery and petroleum capabilities |
| 5. PowerGrid and More Red | Electrical generation, conductive casings, wiring, motors, sensors and circuits |
| 6. Aerospace | Rocket structures, thermal materials, guidance, propellants, life support and space processing |

The primitive workshop should be useful before dimensional expeditions: shelter, cooking, temperature preparation, rustic storage, boats, minecarts, ziplines, gliders and simple wooden ships. Local chutes and hoppers are not a requesting network. Cave starts should be able to gather primitive materials without a guaranteed onboarding cache.

Independent material branches create reasons to travel:

- Two different suitable underground rock families make refractory compound for Seared entry; starter metallurgy is not unlocked by a Font visit.
- Skyroot anchors water and wind machinery.
- Nether Brick anchors powered grinding and separation apparatus.
- Growable Certus anchors scalable routing intelligence.
- Aether, Nether and Bumblezone resources attach to particular themed capabilities, rather than serving as universal dimensional proof tokens.

### Machines are fitted structures

Machine, Toughened, Precision, Airtight, Electrical and Space assemblies represent physical structure. Small devices, open frames, bearings, controls and compact vehicle modules use appropriate manufactured parts, not an entire cubic machine block hidden inside them. A full apparatus assembly belongs where the machine's housing actually warrants it; the recovered design kept one Space assembly in each Air Liquefier.

### Transport is an engineering branch

Eureka/VS ships, Clockwork aircraft, Trackwork vehicles and Create trains supplement the ordinary factory spine. Primitive wooden ships and rough mechanical experiments can exist early. Precision controls, pressure systems and electrical sensors develop later. Aether materials distinguish stable high-performance flight, not the bare possibility of a ship or low-performance aircraft.

The Rail Beetle is practical baseline machinery, not a useless shell awaiting advanced upgrades: a fuel engine, survey, rail laying, bridge work and a short consist. Alternative engines and modules broaden its applications. Freight remains physical cargo. Found rail-work sites should tell a material story through parked vehicles, reserves, ruined timbers and an extinguished hearth, rather than only an extra generic loot chest.

## Geology and processing depth

The ordinary world offers eight recognizable deposits and a separate Oil Seep. A deposit's morphology and immediate promise should communicate its broad use before the player learns an exact assay.

| Deposit | Immediate identity |
| --- | --- |
| Hotstone | Dangerous heat and heavy power |
| Copper Bloom | Responsive copper mechanisms |
| Tin Quartz | Productive toolmaking, quartz and later gem-bearing assays |
| Brassroot | Zinc/brass for moving machinery |
| Coal Measures | Stored fuel for sustained work |
| Ironstone | Durable iron and nickel-bearing depth |
| Evaporite Beds | Salt, preservation and fertile chemistry |
| Black Shale | Redstone control, soul contamination and precious traces |

Processing develops recovery rather than merely replacing one furnace with another:

1. Hand String-Mesh Sifting gives early access to canonical primary products.
2. Milling and crushing improve feed recovery.
3. Dry Sifting and waterlogged Sifting distinguish primary recovery from appropriate coproducts.
4. Spouting prepares feeds; it is not itself separation.
5. Pressure processing recovers the complete geological assay.

Nonmetals do not need ceremonial smelting. Physical and magical processing converge on shared products with different costs. Ordinary terrain recovery supplies appropriate bulk constituents, not a concealed metal or precious-gem generator. Geological depth should make the same deposit more interesting over time.

Chemistry similarly follows visible causes: fermentation and oxidation provide acetic uses; sealed pressure work supports sulfuric, hydrochloric and nitric acid routes; gas-state contents require containment. Heat transport, pressure and electricity are related but not interchangeable costs.

## Food, water and valuable places

Food has three separate roles: immediate restoration, temporary nutrition and long-term variety-based maximum health. Diet classifications, per-life development and maximum-health discovery do not collapse into one progression meter.

The Overworld supports exploration with animal food, wild fruit, berries and finite vegetable/rice patches. Picking wild food is not automatically acquiring renewable planting stock. Bumblezone nurseries provide renewable non-space food seed stock; edible produce is not itself a seed.

Rare fruit trees and berry fields are valuable **places**. Their renewable harvest supports returning, settling or building transport. Direct planting and easy propagation should not turn every discovery into an inventory portable farm. Protected forage retains real growth, seasonal, soil and geometry limitations; it is not an indestructible or guaranteed-output resource. Ordinary trees and non-protected plants keep their separate roles.

Decorative flowers distinguish relocation from multiplication. Moving a flower is allowed; multiplying planting resources is centered on Bumblezone. Existing one-use seed stock is not confiscated.

### Nutrition and development complement one another

Six ordinary nutrients offer benefits continuously from the first positive amount, without a deficiency punishment. Nectar and Booze are optional temporary loads, not requirements for dietary completeness.

| Aspect | Life development | Food identity | Geological identity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Impact | Strength: knockback and stagger | Flesh: melee damage | Hotstone |
| Tempo | Hand Speed: attack/use cadence | Nectar: amplification with faster expenditure | Copper Bloom |
| Work | Tool Handling: productive reach | Grain: correct-tool mining speed | Tin Quartz |
| Mobility | Athletics: stepping and breathing | Fruit: movement and swimming | Brassroot |
| Endurance | Endurance: stamina capacity | Tallow: resource conservation | Coal Measures |
| Robustness | Hardiness: knockback resistance | Earth: temperature protection | Ironstone |
| Renewal | Preservation: beneficial-effect preservation | Cream: harmful-effect recovery | Evaporite Beds |
| Control | Marksmanship: projectile handling | Booze: protection with impairment | Black Shale |

These are recurring meanings, not shared state. Tinkers' materials remain outside this identity matrix. Names, morphology, glyphs, readable value structure, motion and sound complement color; hue alone does not carry meaning.

RPG allocations are committed for a life and lost at final death. Their diminishing-return curve rewards specialization without letting one category stand for every benefit. Food variety retains its independent maximum-health progression through death.

Nectar amplifies benefits while accelerating hunger, thirst, stamina expenditure and nutrient consumption. Booze protects while impairing control and work, with blackout at extreme load. Stronger is not automatically better. Nutrition's sustained benefits and higher-level abilities should remain distinct from RPG capabilities rather than duplicating them.

### Drinking and spoilage

Water has a clear dirty/purified distinction. Carrying or equipping a vessel does not purify its contents; treatment converts finite supplies, and transfers preserve quality.

Supported Drinks-slot beverages are paid for before fractional sipping. Hydration, nutrition and timed effects are released proportionally; completion actions such as cleansing are not replayed on every sip. Manual-only special actions remain distinct from passive compatibility. Ordinary plant juice is nourishment and hydration, not an automatic copy of its source fruit's magical effect; named brewed specialties carry those effects.

Rotten produce has a composting role and rotten meat a basic fishing-bait role. Spoilage should create consequences and alternative uses, not just anonymous inventory waste.

Fishing rewards revisiting native region, season, time and weather windows. Regional specialties retain useful food identities through basic cooking. This is not a new hotspot/depletion system or a promise that a weight increase gives the same increase in catches per hour.

## Combat: credible choices, readable threats

Every supported weapon chassis needs credible general combat output. Utility, rarity, difficult manufacture and bonuses against one narrow enemy family do not replace a viable baseline.

- Normalize clean chassis, not every upgraded build. Materials, traits, affixes and execution can create later differences.
- Slow attacks pay out per connection because misses, interruptions and overkill cost more.
- Ordinary cleave is combat texture, not a second damage bar that demands a large baseline tax.
- Generic armor interaction and unconditional damage effects count toward a weapon's effective output; narrow mob bonuses do not.
- Throws, charged strikes, projectiles and counters have separate budgets from ordinary grounded melee.
- Distinct animation identities need viable carriers. Work-first tools can fight well without becoming weapon-first items.
- First- and third-person controls should not unexpectedly change the player's chosen perspective.

The recovered reach-normalization proposal belongs in [Aspirational features](aspirational-features.md#reach-based-melee-recalibration): it was not the active numerical balance.

Ordinary encounters should be readable, counterable and intentionally imperfect. Underground threats express nearby geology, darkness, depth and physical approach. Surface campaigns have geographic movement, scouts and warnings rather than being unexplained spawn-near-player events.

Campaigns should chiefly threaten places where a player has spent real living Survival time, not punish a newly visited region because the world is old. Familiarity persists across visits without accumulating offline. Already physical enemies can still pursue beyond a familiar region.

### Pacing is local, not universal

Where constant trickle makes an event lose meaning, the rhythm is **accumulate → discharge → recover**: pressure builds, a legible event occurs, then a genuine quiet interval follows. Cave surges and warned campaigns suit this structure. Continuous hunger, hydration, heat and machine throughput do not need to become waves. Credited kills release physical spirits immediately rather than waiting for an artificial reward cadence.

## Death: pressure, release and a fresh decision

The desired rhythm is **travel and pressure → danger at Death's Door → release on final death → reinvigoration on respawn**.

Death's Door leaves the player able to move, fight and use inventory while injuries make the danger concrete. It is not a mandatory crawl, bleed-out timer or demand for a multiplayer rescuer. Injuries need treatment; restoring positive health is a separate matter. Catastrophic and explicit special-kill cases can bypass this intermediate state.

Keeping ordinary gear avoids making the next life an obligatory corpse-retrieval trip. Consequences can remain substantial through lost life allocations and XP, injury history, altered encounter state and return to a personal starting place. Ordinary death, a contained arena match and world succession are different events.

The respawn moment should feel like a new choice, with a recognizable return place and sound/particles. The recap gives the life a readable conclusion through cause, injury history, bests and milestones—not an invented reconstruction of unrecorded play or another reward ledger.

## Spirit commerce and emergent society

Spirits are physical consequences of credited kills, delivered through visible homing entities. They are not awarded for reading lessons, completing a checklist or unlocking a Thread. Spawner-origin or already-soulless victims and economy actors do not become a currency exploit.

Eight market identities connect sacred, wicked, arcane, aerial, aqueous, earthen, infernal and Tempo spirits to recognizable traders, outfits, stations and campfires. Eldritch and Umbral remain outside ordinary commerce.

Two economies remain distinct:

- **Wandering traders** bring themed authored goods, spirit prices and finite stock. Visits can follow the player's recent actual spirit acquisitions while retaining a surprise chance. Reopening a menu does not reroll a trader or refill stock.
- **Village residents** barter real goods through local supplies and nearby recipes. Their society is not merely the wandering catalogue presented by stationary villagers.

Camps are real visits with finite lifetimes, not permanent magical vending machines. Purchased campfires are belongings; temporary trader scenery is not free renewable block stock. Shops, resource use and travel should make society emerge materially.

## Exploration rewards belong to their locations

Loot should preserve the identity of a location and its special rewards. Supply, encounter and treasure roles can guide individual tables, but there is no universal bundle budget or automatic compensation for an empty roll.

Quantity changes belong to selected tables and pools, with their actual consumers in mind. A mob-carried reward or helper table is not a chest simply because it uses a chest-like loot context. Existing item state and intentional scarcity matter. Progression-filtered rewards need not be replaced with generic filler, and already-rolled inventories are not silently rewritten.

## Fonts, rare nights and expeditions

Dimensional Fonts are bounded expeditions rather than permanent costless portals. Stored charge, participants and time make preparation and a safe return meaningful. Destination-themed libations can extend a visit through a visible overhead Pourer. Harvesting a natural Font breaks its expedition binding; the unbound object has a separate role beneath the World Condenser.

Fonts should be discoverable as physical places through recognizable courts, destination palettes, maps and occasional guidance. Their materials attach themed dimensions to actual engineering and cultivation capabilities.

Four rare nights create opportunities rather than another progression currency:

| Night | Intended opportunity |
| --- | --- |
| Thin Veil | Sparse surface spirit encounters in suitable forests and marshes |
| Spirit Fair | An extra themed trader visit with ordinary prices and finite stock |
| Wandering Lights | Directional hints toward indexed natural Fonts; players choose a safe route |
| Open Ways | Charge-free Font travel until dawn, followed by normal costs and safe extraction if needed |

These nights follow the Overworld clock and are not skipped by sleeping. They do not replace cave ecology, multiply drops or create event-only progression items. Witching Moon was not implemented.

## Learning without a quest ledger

The player learns through useful action and visible consequences, not an achievement graph or transferred quest rewards.

| Surface | What it teaches |
| --- | --- |
| Loading lessons and Lessons reference | Spoiler-free fundamentals and changed mental models |
| Contextual Threads | A broader rule discovered through actual experience and remembered across a lineage |
| Item hover | Concise facts attached naturally to the item, consistent in inventory and EMI |
| EMI, Ponder and native guides/screens | Exact recipes, apparatus, multiblocks and operation |
| HUD, events and the world | Immediate controls, warnings and context-dependent guidance |
| Death recap and tips | What happened in the life and useful cause-specific advice |

Possessing a machine is not the same discovery as operating it. Cosmetic Thread facsimiles are not currency or progression gates. Player-authored Trace notes remain distinct from curated instruction.

Clear mechanical explanations coexist with restrained lore. A useful tooltip does not need an invented myth. The broader story can imply before explaining: repeated names, equipment, behavior and functioning systems are stronger discoveries than an exposition dump. Other actors can have independent interests and incomplete knowledge.

Thread illustrations show a concrete cause and visible consequence. Their etched/copperplate archive language uses physical focal points, mineral accents and legibility at small size. Card and loading/Lessons scenes exclude humanoid figures, body parts and text baked into art; framing and typography belong to the interface.

## Ordinary lives and persistent lineages

World succession can preserve discoveries, plans and meaningful continuity without making ordinary death a world reset. Schematics are knowledge for rebuilding, not transported inventories or free construction resources. The World Condenser is the bridge to a successor, not evidence that an entropy economy or apocalypse-scale endgame already exists.

The intended expansion—entropy caching, irreversible Condemnation, interacting strategic curses and mechanically grounded cross-world narrative—is preserved separately in [Aspirational features](aspirational-features.md#lineage-endgame-entropy-caching-and-condemnation).

# Ratlantis depths

Ratlantis now has a second expedition layer beneath its native islands. Its water columns extend to a stony floor near Y 6–22 in newly generated ocean chunks. Bedrock and a rock base remain below the water; the ocean is not water all the way to the bottom of the dimension. Dry island columns stay intact so the first trip can still gather pirat wood and marbled cheese for a Courier Lattice.

## Route through the water

The first Ratlantis visit can still establish a small Pretty Pipes network and rat logistics. Once Create machinery is working, return with diving equipment and underwater building supplies. The deep floor contains Ratlantis ore, occasional prismarine coral reefs, and rare flooded marbled-cheese vaults. Vault barrels offer Ratlantis materials and a chance of sponge. Oratchalcum mechanisms and arcane logistics cores remain the material proofs for higher logistics tiers.

Upgrade Aquatic supplies Ratlantis with jellyfish, lionfish, nautilus, thrasher, ammonites, driftwood, and shoreline plants. Its Ratlantis biome tags are scoped to `rats:ratlantis`; they do not edit Thalassophobia, Untamed Wilds, or Depth Director spawning. In Control rejects two thirds of natural Born in Chaos Corpse Fish and Glutton Fish spawn attempts in Ratlantis only. The separate Depth Director ecology owns the wider Overworld waterway danger plan. Ratlantis keeps its native Rats and Ruins content alongside the new aquatic layer.

An underwater arrival keeps the water around its return Font. Four soul-sand corners in the platform create bubble columns to the surface.

Create: Aquatic Ambitions adds the later underwater workshop, including conduit channeling. Its Conduit Cage requires an Oratchalcum Mechanism alongside the native Conduit, prismarine alloy, alloy rods, and Create fluid pipe. That gives the deeper Ratlantis return trip a visible industrial use. Four upstream recipes are removed to preserve exploration and the pack's material routes: prismarine to lapis, prismarine bricks to lapis and copper, suspicious-rock splashing, and the Ender Eye route to a Heart of the Sea. The remaining native recipes provide the aquatic machinery and conduit uses.

The Ratlantis abyss and vaults are features in `ratlantis-logistics`; they affect newly generated chunks only. The ocean feature keeps the existing island layout and Rats ore-bearing stone below the new floor. Existing Ratlantis terrain is not rewritten.

## Validation boundary

The Ratlantis Logistics source runs its repository-local `verifyFull` gate, and the pack content runs Dev checks. Distribution assembly, JAR deployment, and pack-level Dist/Debug tests require a separate explicit request under the workspace policy.

# Initial shared-spawn staging and recovery (generation zero only)

**Not a release or a friends' playtest result.** The preceding ten passing full-pack trials validated only the earlier point-spawn policy, not this nine-chunk revision. Fresh worlds require the final saved biome and the two blocks immediately above it to remain within Cold Sweat's configured habitable base-temperature bounds **and outside `#minecraft:is_ocean`** (including warm and deep frozen oceans); vanilla can place a first-join spectator a block above the saved shared spawn. The saved spawn's chunk and all eight adjacent chunks must each have one qualifying biome column (one sample per chunk, at the neighboring chunk centers). `config/better_world_management-initial-spawn-blacklist.txt` may exclude additional registered biome IDs; missing or empty files cannot permit ocean spawns. This is initial-world policy only; terrain safety is not gated because new players scout in spectator mode. The check does not guarantee contiguous land or comfortable effective Cold Sweat temperature. Source edits do not update an installed mod JAR.

The search spends at most 30 seconds per seed on pre-world screening plus post-start loaded-world selection; intervening Minecraft world startup is not part of that 30-second search counter. The ten-minute overall deadline includes startup, retries, and publication checks. A `screened 1 candidate sites` message means a preliminary column was found, not that a full world or its nine-chunk neighborhood qualified; the attempt number changes only when a random seed is actually rotated.

## Fresh dedicated world

Launch through `./run.sh`, never directly through Forge. With generation zero, an absent `level-name` save is prepared under `.better_world_management/initial-spawn/<run>/universe-<attempt>/bc-initial-<id>`; only loopback network listeners are configured. The raw `level-seed` server-property value is captured before rewriting temporary properties. Empty input permits distinct random-seed attempts; nonempty or unavailable input never permits seed rotation. The candidate persists `level.dat` without a server-tick flush of all chunks; normal shutdown must flush the closed save. Only then are its saved spawn/seed and V4 parametric neighborhood receipt (at the existing `initial-spawn-attempt-v2.tsv` path) checked before atomic no-replace promotion. The supervisor restores the *original bytes* of `server.properties` and reopens the final world. All rejected candidates stay protected under the run directory.

A fresh server without the companion staged mod refuses preparation: **do not replace it with a direct Forge launch**. When a world already exists, the regular world launch and successor transaction remain separate from this flow.

## Fresh single-player world

The normal Create World screen retains its generation display. Candidate saves live outside `saves/` under `.better_world_management/initial-spawn/<transaction>/staging/`. The client waits without making a local connection, checks a verified receipt against the saved `level.dat`, stops the integrated server, moves the closed candidate into `saves/` without replacing an existing name, and only then reloads it. Failed attempts are retained under the transaction directory. An explicit seed with no eligible non-blacklisted biome fails closed; there is no unchecked vanilla fallback.

## Interrupted or failed preparation

- A failed or interrupted candidate is **not** a playable world. Never move an attempt folder into the final world name merely because `initial-spawn-v1.tsv` says `pending` or `resolved`.
- A dedicated `active-v2.tsv` marker blocks the next launch. Inspect the protected transaction and any player data/login marker first; check whether the final world name already exists. The run directory holds `server.properties.before` with the original bytes. If an abrupt kill interrupted restoration, restore those bytes before deliberately resolving the marker. Do **not** delete an established or inhabited world to retry.
- In single-player, the protected `staging/` and `attempt-N/` folders are retained for inspection; never open one as a normal save. A world already in `saves/` is not rotated or reselected.
- Protected `attempt-N-supervisor-v2.tsv` (dedicated) and `creation-intent-v2.tsv` (single-player) preserve original seed provenance even when startup fails before world creation. Raw seed input is base64-encoded there; encoding is **not encryption**. `initial-spawn-attempt-v2.tsv` is a restricted file: new V4 receipts include the seed, provenance, target, status, final spawn coordinates, biome ID, registered base temperature, loaded temperature bounds, and the nine sampled chunk coordinates, biome IDs, and base temperatures; V3 and historical V2 receipts remain readable for established worlds. The old `initial-spawn-v1.tsv` retains the status string for existing pack readers. A failed attempt has a separate `initial-spawn-outcome-v2.tsv` with a bounded reason; `kind=retry` permits another **random-seed** attempt only, while technical failures and deadlines do not.

No automatic recovery operation deletes or overwrites a published save. Review protected receipts and restore backups before any manual cleanup. To roll back the feature, restore the previous mod and supervisor/client-hook artifacts through the normal release process; **never roll back, delete, or regenerate a published player world**. Owner/unit/native tests require separate human approval to run; packaging, deployment, and publication use their own gates.
